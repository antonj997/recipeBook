Recipebook shopping product catalogue

Contains information from Open Food Facts contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0).
Individual contents are available under the Database Contents License (DbCL 1.0).

Source: https://static.openfoodfacts.org/data/taxonomies/categories.full.json
The retrieval date, source checksum and provenance are included in the derived data file.

Changes: Swedish/English selection, normalization, shop-department mapping,
singular and cheese-compound aliases, conflict filtering, pruning of names
already recognized by Recipebook rules, and reviewed Swedish product names.
The derived catalogue and its additions are available under ODbL 1.0.
This catalogue is not a complete ICA stock list or an ICA integration.

Derived data: https://antonj997.github.io/recipeBook/data/shopping-products.json

Selected grocery names were shortened and given plural aliases. Department
assignments are Recipebook's own supermarket mapping, not official nutrition
classifications. Original source names and food IDs are retained in
scripts/shopping-catalog/swedish-aliases.json in the public repository.
No nutritional values were imported or modified. Source checksum and attribution
are included in the derived database. The existing Open Food Facts catalogue
and the separately maintained Swedish cheese names remain in place.
