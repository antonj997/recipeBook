# Recipebook

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

A personal recipe collection for saving independent copies of recipes, browsing them, and cooking from them on mobile or desktop.

## Capabilities and constraints

Existing Angular components, reactive recipe form, website import with photo processing, and Dexie storage are retained. The owner is learning the code and wants small understandable changes. No automated tests or unnecessary dependencies. Preserve saved recipes, backup behavior and imports.

## Brand commitments

The September 22 redesign explicitly uses the supplied Anthropic screenshots: off-white and near-black, thin rules, whitespace, muted beige, green and blue accents, simple food doodles. Minimal factual labels; no slogans. Home search and add actions; settings icon at top right. Responsive carousel with snapping, soft edges and optional glass distortion.
